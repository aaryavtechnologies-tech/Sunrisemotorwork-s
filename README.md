## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript
- **Bundler:** Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Deep Black `#050505` & Rich Gold `#F5B800` theme)
- **Routing:** React Router DOM (7 dedicated pages)
- **Icons:** Lucide React + Custom SVG Brand Badges
- **SEO & Meta:** JSON-LD LocalBusiness Schema for AutoRepair

---

## 📑 Website Pages
1. **Home (`/`):** Full-width automotive hero with interactive 4-bay showcase switcher, trust section, 8 featured services, multi-brand strip, about preview, 6 why-choose-us cards, 4-step workflow, and WhatsApp CTAs.
2. **About Us (`/about`):** Business history since 2010, founder introduction (Keharsingh), workshop facilities, brand heritage badge, and "Our Approach" guiding principles.
3. **Our Services (`/services`):** Complete catalog with category filter tabs and real-time search covering General Maintenance, Mechanical Repairs, Electrical & Diagnostics, AC Services, Detailing, and Additional Services (EV & Genuine Parts).
4. **Car Brands (`/brands`):** Multi-brand engineering coverage distinguishing German/European Luxury Marques (Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Porsche, Jaguar, Land Rover, Volvo, Lexus) and Indian/Japanese Marques (Maruti, Tata, Mahindra, Hyundai, Toyota, Kia, etc.).
5. **Service Plans (`/service-plans`):** 5 structured packages (Petrol General, Petrol Full, Diesel General, CNG General, CNG Full) clearly distinguishing Replacement, Cleaning, Top-Up, and Inspection with official vehicle inspection disclaimer.
6. **Service Locations (`/locations`):** Verified pickup & drop coverage corridors with interactive cards and embedded Google Maps for the Diva (E) workshop.
7. **Contact Us (`/contact`):** Click-to-call (+91 9619313180), WhatsApp chat, Instagram link, driving directions, 8-field interactive service request form, and FAQ accordion.
